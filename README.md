## Why Version Control Matters for IT Operations

- **Configuration Management**: Track changes to scripts, playbooks, and configs
- **Rollback Safety**: Revert to known-good states quickly
- **Collaboration**: Multiple team members work on infrastructure safely
- **Audit Trail**: Know who changed what and when
- **Documentation as Code**: Version your runbooks and procedures
